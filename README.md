One-line pitch for make.fun's "A Day of One-Line Pitches" bounty ($5 in USDC).
The entry is a single sentence that would convince a friend who has never
coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun.

## The pitch

> Tell make.fun's AI agent your app idea in plain words, and by tonight you'll have a real working app to show your friends — no code, no cost to enter, just the idea you've always had finally built.

197 characters (limit: 240).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the pitch — and nothing else, no trailing newline — to
`/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that variable is set).
